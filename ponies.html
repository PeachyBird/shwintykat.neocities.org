'Fakie' or 'Phony' is a term coined by My Little Pony collectors for off-brand toys trying to get in on the popularity of My Little Ponies.
Some collectors have even taken to collecting fakies.

Cabbage Patch Magic Meadow Pony Guide
In 1993, Hasbro produced a line of Cabbage Patch ponies called Magic Meadow Ponies
These ponies are often confused with My Little Pony because they have the same manufacturer (Hasbro) and are both colorful pony toys.
