I've been collecting unicorn & fanciful horse figurines for nearly two decades!
I do not attempt to repair or re-paint my figurines and very rarely purchase them new
(unless they really stick out to me!) as I love the idea that they're being loved again.

Giraffes are also included here, as I consider them to be real-life unicorns!
