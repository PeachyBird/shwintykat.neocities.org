The Secret Laboratory is an area on Neopets that allows for random stats changes, as well as species, gender, and colour changes for your pets!
Once per day, those who have completed the full secret lab map can choose one pet to zap and potentially change!

This is where I will begin logging species & color changes for my current lab rats on vanilla Neopets.
